Argentine Assets in Foreign Successions

We assess whether an Argentine succession proceeding, recognition step or local registration is required to deal with assets located in Argentina.

Key questions before starting

The deceased’s last domicile is a central connecting factor. Argentine courts may also have jurisdiction regarding real estate located in Argentina. The precise route depends on the assets, the foreign proceeding, applicable treaties and the documents already obtained abroad.

Not necessarily. A foreign decision may require recognition, and Argentine real estate remains subject to Argentine law and registration requirements. The foreign order, the heirs’ status and the local assets must be examined before choosing a procedure.

Many steps may be coordinated remotely through properly prepared powers and documentation, although the form, authentication, apostille and translation requirements depend on where the documents are issued and what must be done in Argentina.

Real estate, vehicles, bank accounts, company interests, receivables and debts should be distinguished. Not every asset follows the same jurisdictional or registration rules, so an accurate inventory is more useful than assuming one foreign decision resolves the entire estate.

Common issues include inconsistent names, missing civil-status records, unclear ownership, documents that are not final or authenticated, unreported heirs, debts and disagreement over sale or distribution. A preliminary review can reduce avoidable filings.
