Key questions before starting

A return proceeding may arise when a child under sixteen was habitually resident in another country and is allegedly removed to or retained in Argentina in breach of rights of custody. Nationality alone does not decide the issue. Habitual residence, the circumstances of the move, consent, custody rights and the applicable convention must all be examined.

These proceedings are designed to be expeditious, and delay can affect evidence, available measures and the legal analysis. A prompt review helps identify the correct international instrument, the location of the child and the documents that can demonstrate habitual residence and custody rights.
